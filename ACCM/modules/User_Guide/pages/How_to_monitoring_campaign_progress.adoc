= How to monitor campaign progress

== Introduction

This section provides the steps required to monitor the progress of the campaigns.

== Monitor the progress of a campaign

To monitor the progress of a campaign using the {app-short-name} WebUI, you must execute the following steps:

. Click the "CAMPAIGNS" option on the menu bar displayed on top of the screen.
+
image::CAMPAIGNS_menu.png[align="center"]

. All the campaigns will be listed:
+
image::CAMPAIGNS_menuSection.png[align="center"]

. Locate the campaign to monitor, and click the Detail button on the row.
+
image::CAMPAIGNSSection_detail.png[align="center"]

. The general progress for the campaign is shown:
+
.General progress for success campaign
image::CAMPAIGNSSection_GeneralProgress.png[align="center"]

. Locate the cluster to monitor, and click the Details button on the row.
+
image::CAMPAIGNSSection_detail.png[align="center"]

. The general progress for the selected cluster is shown:
+
.Progress for a success cluster
image::CAMPAIGNSSection_clusterDetails2.png[align="center"]

. In case of error, the cause is shown at cluster level, and every step has its own cause:
+
image::CAMPAIGNSSection_clusterDetailsError2New.png[align="center"]

[NOTE]
====
While Download and Upgrade progress are always visible in the Progress view, Rollback progress is shown dynamically only when a rollback operation is underway.

image::CAMPAIGNSSection_clusterDetails3.png[align="center"]
====

== Monitor element's operations

To monitor the operations of an element using the {app-short-name} WebUI, you must execute the following steps:

. Click the "CAMPAIGNS" option on the menu bar displayed on top of the screen.
+
image::CAMPAIGNS_menu.png[align="center"]

. All the campaigns will be listed:
+
image::CAMPAIGNS_menuSection.png[align="center"]

. Locate the campaign to monitor, and click the Detail button on the row.
+
image::CAMPAIGNSSection_detail.png[align="center"]

. The general progress for the campaign is shown:
+
.General progress for success campaign
image::CAMPAIGNSSection_GeneralProgress.png[align="center"]

. Locate the cluster to monitor, and click the Details button on the row.
+
image::CAMPAIGNSSection_detail.png[align="center"]

. The general progress for the selected cluster is shown:
+
.Progress for a success cluster
image::CAMPAIGNSSection_clusterDetails2.png[align="center"]

. Locate the element to monitor, and click the Detail button on the row.
+
image::CAMPAIGNSSection_detail.png[align="center"]

. The timeline of the operations for the element are shown.
+
image::CAMPAIGNSSection_clusterDetailsElementOperations.png[align="center"]
+
If an operation lacks start and end dates while the campaign is in a final state, it indicates that the operation was not executed on that element.
+
image::CAMPAIGNSSection_clusterDetailsElementOperationsError.png[align="center"]

+
[NOTE]
====
Rollback operation is displayed dynamically only when a rollback is executed on an element.

image::CAMPAIGNSSection_clusterDetailsElementOperationsRollback.png[align="center"]
====
